Policy Research and Strategic Documents

This library introduces policy studies, strategic proposals and research materials related to Goyang's bid to host the Global AI Hub.

The materials address site-selection criteria, international cooperation, initial settlement, operational support, public-interest AI pilot projects and Goyang's institutional capabilities.

Document Availability

English summaries are provided for selected materials. Full-text documents will be linked after publication and upload.

Research proposals should not be interpreted as official commitments by the United Nations, international organizations or government agencies.
