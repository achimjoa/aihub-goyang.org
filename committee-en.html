About the Committee

The Goyang Citizens' Committee for Hosting the Global AI Hub in Gyeonggi Province is a civic coalition established to support Goyang Special City's bid to host the Global AI Hub.

The Committee brings together civic organizations, professionals, community leaders and citizens to promote cooperation among Goyang Special City, Gyeonggi Province, public institutions, universities, businesses and civil society.

Our Vision

Our vision is to help Goyang become an internationally connected city where public-interest artificial intelligence contributes to human well-being, sustainable development and the protection of vulnerable populations.

We believe that hosting the Global AI Hub should create lasting value for international organizations, Goyang's citizens and the global community.

Our Three Strategic Priorities

1. Initial Settlement

Support international organizations in establishing their operations through suitable office facilities, housing, transportation, healthcare, education and administrative assistance.

2. Operational Support

Connect local institutions, universities, companies and civic organizations with the practical activities of international organizations.

3. Shared Growth

Support international organizations in achieving their missions while helping Goyang develop into a city specializing in international cooperation.

Our Guiding Principles

Practical Implementation

Prioritize achievable plans, clear responsibilities, realistic resources and verifiable results.

Public Interest

Promote the responsible use of artificial intelligence for humanity and sustainable development.

International Cooperation

Build cooperation among international organizations, governments, academic institutions, businesses and civil society.
