About the Citizens' Committee

The Goyang Citizens' Committee for Hosting the Global AI Hub in Gyeonggi Province brings together citizens, civic organizations, professionals and community leaders to support Goyang's bid to host the Global AI Hub.

Our mission goes beyond attracting international organizations. We seek to build a city that contributes to solving global challenges through public-interest artificial intelligence, international cooperation and sustainable development.

Our guiding principle is to develop practical, achievable plans supported by real capabilities and verifiable evidence.

What Is the Global AI Hub?

The Global AI Hub is envisioned as an international cooperation platform that uses artificial intelligence to address major challenges facing humanity.

These challenges include public health, food insecurity, climate change, forced displacement, migration, labor-market transformation, disaster preparedness, child protection and sustainable development.

Rather than simply providing office space, the proposed hub would support international organizations in jointly designing, developing, evaluating and deploying public-interest AI solutions.

Why Goyang?

Goyang offers a combination of metropolitan accessibility, healthcare institutions, international convention facilities, educational resources, environmental assets and an active civil society.

Strategic Location

Convenient access to Seoul and the wider metropolitan region.

International Convention Facilities

KINTEX provides facilities for international conferences, exhibitions and cooperation events.

Healthcare Infrastructure

The National Cancer Center, National Health Insurance Service Ilsan Hospital and other medical institutions provide a foundation for potential public-health cooperation.

Environmental Assets

The Ramsar-listed Janghang Wetland offers opportunities for environmental monitoring and climate-related AI research.

Smart City Infrastructure

Urban management and disaster-response systems provide potential foundations for public-interest AI pilot projects.

Civic Participation

Citizens and civic organizations are working together to support Goyang's Global AI Hub initiative.

Our Three-Stage Strategy

1. Initial Settlement

Support international organizations in establishing their operations through suitable facilities, housing, transportation, healthcare and education.

2. Operational Support

Connect local institutions, universities, companies and civic organizations to the practical work of international organizations.

3. Shared Growth

Contribute to global development goals while helping Goyang evolve into a city specializing in international cooperation.
