Our Vision

To develop Goyang Special City into a center for international cooperation where artificial intelligence supports sustainable development, human well-being, and solutions to global challenges.

1. What Is the Global AI Hub?

The proposed Global AI Hub is envisioned as an international cooperation platform connecting the expertise of international organizations with advances in artificial intelligence.

Its purpose is to explore how AI can contribute to addressing complex global challenges, including public health, food security, climate change, environmental protection, humanitarian assistance, migration, employment, education, and disaster resilience.

The initiative seeks to encourage cooperation among international organizations, governments, academic institutions, research centers, businesses, and civil society.

International Cooperation

Facilitating dialogue, knowledge exchange, and cooperation across institutions and countries.

AI Research and Innovation

Exploring responsible AI applications for public-interest challenges through research and collaboration.

Practical Solutions

Supporting opportunities for pilot projects, technology assessment, and evidence-based approaches to global challenges.

Capacity Development

Exploring training, professional exchange, and international learning opportunities.

2. Why Goyang Special City?

Goyang Special City is located in Gyeonggi Province, within the Seoul metropolitan region of the Republic of Korea.

Its international exhibition and convention infrastructure, healthcare institutions, urban services, and environmental assets provide a foundation for exploring the development of an international cooperation hub.

Strategic Metropolitan Location

Goyang benefits from its location within the Seoul metropolitan region, offering access to major economic, academic, and institutional networks.

KINTEX Convention Infrastructure

KINTEX provides facilities for international exhibitions, conferences, and professional exchanges.

This infrastructure could support international meetings, forums, and collaborative events associated with the proposed hub.

Healthcare and Research Potential

Goyang is home to the National Cancer Center and other healthcare institutions.

These assets offer opportunities to explore cooperation in public health, medical research, and responsible AI applications.

Environmental Assets

The Janghang Wetland and surrounding ecological resources provide opportunities for environmental education, research, and sustainability initiatives.

Living Environment

Goyang offers residential areas, healthcare services, educational facilities, cultural amenities, and urban infrastructure.

Specific requirements for international personnel and their families would need to be assessed through consultation.

Regional Development Opportunities

International cooperation could create opportunities for research, professional employment, business development, and related services.

Actual outcomes would depend on the scale and structure of future partnerships.

A Location for International Cooperation and Shared Growth

Goyang's existing assets provide a basis for exploring a Global AI Hub that supports both international cooperation and regional development.

3. International Organizations and Potential Areas of Cooperation

The initiative identifies nine international organizations whose areas of expertise are relevant to potential AI-enabled international cooperation.

WHO

World Health Organization

Public health, disease prevention, health systems, and potential AI applications for health information analysis.

WFP

World Food Programme

Food security, humanitarian assistance, supply-chain resilience, and potential AI-supported food crisis analysis.

UNEP

United Nations Environment Programme

Climate change, environmental protection, biodiversity, and potential AI applications for environmental monitoring.

UNHCR

United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees

Refugee protection, forced displacement, humanitarian assistance, and responsible data analysis for protection-related needs.

IOM

International Organization for Migration

Migration, displacement, humanitarian support, and potential digital tools for improving access to migration-related services.

ILO

International Labour Organization

Employment, labor rights, skills development, and analysis of the effects of AI on the future of work.

ITU

International Telecommunication Union

Digital connectivity, telecommunications, emergency communications, and early-warning technologies.

UNICEF

United Nations Children's Fund

Child protection, education, digital inclusion, and responsible AI applications that safeguard children's rights.

UNDP

United Nations Development Programme

Sustainable development, crisis response, institutional capacity, and potential AI applications for development planning.

Important Clarification

These organizations are identified as potential areas of engagement based on their respective mandates.

Their inclusion does not imply that any organization has agreed to participate in, relocate to, or establish an office at the proposed Global AI Hub.

Any future cooperation would require appropriate consultation, institutional decisions, and formal agreements.

4. Three-Stage Development Strategy

The proposed approach considers the practical requirements of international organizations and other potential partners.

Stage 1 — Initial Establishment

Exploring suitable conditions for the initial establishment of participating organizations and partner institutions.

Potential considerations include office facilities, transportation, housing, healthcare, education, and other essential services.

Stage 2 — Operational Support

Exploring cooperation among relevant public institutions, universities, research organizations, businesses, and civil society.

Potential activities include joint research, international meetings, AI pilot projects, and professional exchanges.

Stage 3 — Shared and Sustainable Growth

Seeking long-term cooperation that contributes to the mandates of participating international organizations while creating opportunities for regional innovation, employment, and economic development.

Initial Establishment → Operational Support → Shared Growth

Specific institutional arrangements, funding, facilities, and support measures would be subject to consultation and approval by the relevant authorities.

5. About the Citizens' Promotion Committee

The Citizens' Promotion Committee for Attracting the Global AI Hub to Goyang Special City is a civic initiative seeking to build public awareness and support for the proposed hub.

The Committee aims to promote public discussion, encourage citizen participation, and communicate the potential benefits of international cooperation and AI-driven innovation.

Its activities include public information, citizen engagement, policy discussions, and outreach related to the Global AI Hub initiative.

Our Commitment

To promote transparent, responsible, and constructive engagement in support of international cooperation and the future development of Goyang Special City.

The proposed Global AI Hub seeks to encourage dialogue with organizations and institutions interested in responsible AI, international cooperation, and sustainable development.

Potential areas of engagement include:

Policy Dialogue
