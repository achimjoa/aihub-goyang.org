1. What Is the Global AI Hub?

The proposed Global AI Hub is envisioned as an international cooperation platform where international organizations, governments, universities, research institutions, businesses, and civil society can explore ways to use artificial intelligence for the common good.

Artificial intelligence is a key technology transforming industries and economies. It also has the potential to contribute to addressing global challenges, including disease, hunger, climate change, disasters, and educational inequality.

The Global AI Hub initiative seeks to connect these possibilities with international cooperation, public policy, research and development, and practical applications.

The central purpose of the Global AI Hub is not simply to bring international organizations together in one location, but to encourage international cooperation and collective problem-solving through responsible applications of AI.

2. Why Is a Global AI Hub Needed?

Many of the challenges facing humanity cannot be addressed effectively by a single country or institution acting alone.

Public health emergencies, food insecurity, climate change, disaster prevention and response, forced displacement, migration, changes in labor markets, and educational inequality require international cooperation, information exchange, and scientific innovation.

AI can support the analysis of large volumes of information, help identify emerging risks, and assist decision-making in public policy and practical operations.

However, translating AI capabilities into meaningful public benefits requires reliable data, effective institutional cooperation, ethical safeguards, skilled professionals, and sustainable operating arrangements.

The proposed Global AI Hub seeks to bring these elements together through an international cooperation framework.

3. Six Core Functions of the Global AI Hub

01. International Cooperation Platform

Facilitate dialogue, information exchange, and potential cooperation among international organizations, governments, research institutions, and businesses.

02. Public-Interest AI Research

Explore collaborative research and development of AI applications that address public challenges in healthcare, environmental protection, disaster resilience, education, and related fields.

03. International Joint Projects

Identify opportunities for collaborative research, pilot projects, and practical applications by connecting international needs with local technological capabilities.

04. Professional Development

Explore education, training, and professional exchange opportunities for people working at the intersection of AI and international cooperation.

05. Industry and Entrepreneurship

Explore connections among AI businesses, research institutions, and international cooperation needs to support technology demonstration, entrepreneurship, and industrial innovation.

06. Responsible AI

Promote consideration of privacy, safety, fairness, human rights, and accountability in the development and use of AI technologies.

4. Priority Areas for International Cooperation

The Global AI Hub initiative seeks to explore cooperation in fields relevant to the mandates and expertise of international organizations and specialized institutions.

Public Health

Disease prevention, infectious disease response, health information analysis, and strengthening public health capacity.

Food and Agriculture

Food security, agricultural productivity, climate adaptation, and sustainable agriculture.

Climate and Environment

Climate action, environmental data analysis, ecosystem protection, and sustainable development.

Disaster Risk and Safety

Disaster risk assessment, early-warning systems, emergency response, and reducing disaster impacts.

Refugees and Migration

Humanitarian assistance, migration-related information, protection of vulnerable populations, and international cooperation.

Employment and Education

Future employment, vocational training, educational accessibility, and reducing digital inequalities.

These areas describe potential fields of cooperation. They do not imply confirmed participation by any international organization or approval of specific projects.

5. The Global AI Hub and Goyang Special City

Goyang Special City is located in Gyeonggi Province, within the Seoul metropolitan region of the Republic of Korea.

The city offers metropolitan accessibility, international convention infrastructure, healthcare institutions, educational and cultural facilities, and an established urban environment.

These assets provide a foundation for exploring Goyang's potential as a center for public-interest AI and international cooperation.

If international cooperation expands in practice, new opportunities could emerge in research and development, business activities, professional services, and the regional economy.

Important long-term objectives include professional development for young people, high-quality employment, technological innovation among local businesses, and expanded international exchange.

Attracting the proposed Global AI Hub represents a potential strategy for strengthening Goyang's future growth opportunities, employment prospects, and regional economic development.

Actual economic outcomes would depend on the scale of future institutional participation, the nature of cooperative activities, and the operational arrangements ultimately established.

7. Citizen Participation and Shared Responsibility

The proposed Global AI Hub is not solely a matter for public authorities.

It is also an opportunity for citizens, local communities, universities, research institutions, businesses, and professionals to contribute to discussions about Goyang's future.

The Citizens' Promotion Committee encourages voluntary public participation in the initiative.

Activities include online expressions of support, Committee registration, supporting membership, and public awareness campaigns.

8. Institutional Status

The Global AI Hub in Goyang is a proposed initiative supported by the Citizens' Promotion Committee.

The Committee's proposals do not constitute an official site-selection decision, an approved government project, or a commitment by any international organization.

Future institutional participation, funding, location decisions, and operational arrangements would require appropriate consultation and formal approval by the responsible authorities.

The initiative seeks to promote responsible international cooperation, constructive policy dialogue, and practical preparation for future opportunities.
