The Global AI Hub Vision and Goyang's Initiative

What Is the Global AI Hub?

The Global AI Hub is envisioned as an international cooperation platform that harnesses artificial intelligence to address some of humanity's most pressing challenges.

These challenges include public health, food insecurity, climate change, forced displacement, migration, labor-market transformation, disaster preparedness, child protection and sustainable development.

The proposed hub would go beyond providing offices or research facilities. It would bring together international organizations, governments, universities, research institutions, businesses and civil society to jointly design, develop, evaluate and deploy public-interest AI solutions.

The Global AI Hub is not simply about constructing buildings. It is about creating an international cooperation system capable of addressing real-world challenges.

A Three-Layer Framework for Public AI Cooperation

A policy vision articulated by National Assembly Member Cha Ji-ho emphasizes designing the Global AI Hub around its functions and operating model before determining its physical requirements.

Layer 1

Governance, Evaluation and Procurement

Develop institutional frameworks, safety and evaluation mechanisms, and procurement standards for trustworthy public-interest AI.

Layer 2

Domain-Specific Co-Design

Jointly design AI initiatives with international organizations across healthcare, food security, climate, migration and other humanitarian fields.

Layer 3

Development, Localization, Validation and Scaling

Jointly develop AI solutions, adapt them to local contexts, validate their effectiveness and support wider international adoption.

This framework is a policy proposal, not a formally adopted operating structure of the Global AI Hub.

Proposed Flagship Pilot Projects

Goyang's strategic studies identify two priority areas for potential international cooperation.

1. AI-Enabled Primary Healthcare

Explore how public-interest AI can improve access to essential healthcare services, particularly in underserved communities.

Potential applications include infectious disease surveillance, health-risk detection, clinical decision support and public health information.

Medical safety, privacy, clinical validation and appropriate human oversight would be essential requirements.

2. AI for Complex Crisis Response

Explore integrated approaches to interconnected crises involving climate disasters, food insecurity, infectious diseases, displacement and economic shocks.

Potential applications include risk forecasting, early warning, humanitarian resource coordination and recovery planning.

Implementation would require cooperation among relevant international organizations and local institutions.

Goyang's Proposed Contribution

Goyang aims to connect its healthcare, education, cultural, environmental, transportation and convention resources to the practical needs of international organizations.

Initial Settlement

Facilitate the establishment of international organizations through appropriate office facilities, housing, transportation, healthcare and education services.

Operational Support

Build cooperation among universities, medical institutions, businesses, public agencies and civil society to support practical operations and potential pilot projects.

Shared Growth

Contribute to the missions of international organizations and the Sustainable Development Goals, while supporting Goyang's development as a city specializing in international cooperation.

Our priority is practical feasibility: identify existing assets, secure institutional cooperation, verify implementation conditions and begin with achievable projects.

Civic Participation and International Cooperation

The success of the Global AI Hub would depend not only on governments and international organizations, but also on meaningful participation by local communities.

The Goyang Citizens' Committee seeks to contribute through public engagement, policy recommendations, cooperation with local institutions and support for international partnerships.
