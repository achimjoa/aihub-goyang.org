The Journey Toward the Global AI Hub

This timeline presents major developments related to the Global AI Hub initiative in the Republic of Korea, together with activities undertaken in support of attracting the proposed Hub to Goyang Special City.

Citizen participation, policy preparation, and institutional cooperation are important foundations for future international collaboration.

Major Milestones

Future Priorities

Preparing for the Site-Selection Evaluation

Review official government announcements and evaluation criteria, and present Goyang's competitiveness and implementation plans using objective supporting evidence.

Preparing for the Initial Establishment of International Organizations

Develop practical proposals for office facilities, residential conditions, healthcare, education, transportation, and other services that may be required by participating institutions.

Preparing Public-Interest AI Pilot Projects

Examine the feasibility of potential cooperation projects in AI-enabled essential healthcare and responses to complex crises.

Identify potential partner institutions and assess the requirements for implementation.

Important Notice

Future schedules may change according to official government announcements and institutional decisions.

This page summarizes major developments and activities. It does not imply that all future plans or institutional arrangements have been officially confirmed.
