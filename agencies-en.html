International Cooperation for Humanity

The Global AI Hub is envisioned as an international cooperation platform that brings together international organizations, governments, universities, research institutions, businesses and civil society.

Its purpose is to develop and apply public-interest artificial intelligence to address global challenges and advance the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Goyang is exploring ways to support international organizations through its healthcare, environmental, disaster-management, education, welfare and civic resources.

The following areas represent proposed cooperation opportunities. Specific institutional participation and project arrangements remain subject to consultation and official confirmation.

WHO

World Health Organization

Key Areas
Primary healthcare, public health, infectious disease preparedness.

Potential Cooperation in Goyang
AI-enabled primary healthcare, public-health monitoring and early warning, drawing on the National Cancer Center and local healthcare institutions.

WFP

World Food Programme

Key Areas
Food security, hunger response and emergency food assistance.

Potential Cooperation in Goyang
AI applications for food supply chains, nutrition-risk analysis and food distribution, drawing on local agriculture, school meals and community food-support systems.

UNEP

United Nations Environment Programme

Key Areas
Climate action, environmental protection and sustainable development.

Potential Cooperation in Goyang
AI-based ecological monitoring, climate-risk analysis and environmental data applications using Janghang Wetland and local climate initiatives.

UNHCR

United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees

Key Areas
Refugee protection, forced displacement and humanitarian assistance.

Potential Cooperation in Goyang
Multilingual settlement-support AI, humanitarian information services and cooperation drawing on community welfare, education and healthcare resources.

IOM

International Organization for Migration

Key Areas
Migration, disaster displacement and migration policy.

Potential Cooperation in Goyang
AI-assisted displacement information management, multilingual disaster communications and migration-policy research, building on local experience.

ILO

International Labour Organization

Key Areas
Labor rights, employment and labor-market transitions.

Potential Cooperation in Goyang
AI analysis of labor-market changes, employment-risk early warning and workforce transition support.

ITU

International Telecommunication Union

Key Areas
Telecommunications, disaster communications and early-warning systems.

Potential Cooperation in Goyang
AI-enabled disaster early warning, emergency communications and urban safety applications using smart-city and disaster-response infrastructure.

UNICEF

United Nations Children's Fund

Key Areas
Child protection, education, health and child welfare.

Potential Cooperation in Goyang
AI-supported early identification of risks to children, educational continuity and child-protection services.

UNDP

United Nations Development Programme

Key Areas
Complex crisis response, recovery and sustainable development.

Potential Cooperation in Goyang
Integrated AI analysis for complex crises, community resilience and recovery, connecting disaster management, economic development and social services.

Goyang's Cooperation Strategy

Goyang seeks to connect its existing institutions, infrastructure and civic capabilities with the missions of international organizations.

Two potential flagship initiatives are AI-enabled primary healthcare and AI-supported complex crisis response.

The proposed approach emphasizes practical cooperation, achievable implementation plans and verifiable evidence.
