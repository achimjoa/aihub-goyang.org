1. Global AI Hub and International Organizations

The proposed Global AI Hub is envisioned as an international cooperation platform connecting the expertise of international organizations with advances in artificial intelligence.

The initiative seeks to explore how responsible AI applications could contribute to addressing global challenges in public health, food security, climate change, environmental protection, humanitarian assistance, migration, employment, education, disaster resilience, and sustainable development.

International organizations, governments, universities, research institutions, businesses, and civil society could contribute their respective expertise to collaborative activities.

The objective of the proposed Global AI Hub is not simply to attract offices of international organizations.

Its broader purpose is to explore meaningful cooperation that connects international expertise with AI research and innovation to address shared global challenges.

Important clarification: The nine organizations introduced below are identified as potential areas of engagement based on their mandates. Their inclusion does not indicate confirmed participation, relocation, office establishment, or partnership.

2. Nine International Organizations and Potential Areas of AI Cooperation

Organization 01

WHO

World Health Organization

Public Health and Healthcare

WHO is the United Nations specialized agency responsible for international public health.

Its work includes disease prevention, health emergency preparedness, health systems strengthening, and international health cooperation.

Potential areas of AI cooperation could include disease surveillance, early detection of health risks, public health data analysis, and improved access to healthcare services.

Such cooperation would require appropriate safeguards for privacy, health data governance, safety, and equity.

Organization 02

WFP

World Food Programme

Food Security and Supply Chains

WFP is a United Nations humanitarian organization addressing hunger and food insecurity.

Its activities include emergency food assistance, nutrition support, and strengthening resilience against food-related crises.

Potential AI applications could include food insecurity forecasting, supply-chain risk analysis, early warning of food crises, and improved humanitarian logistics planning.

Organization 03

UNEP

United Nations Environment Programme

Climate Change and Environment

UNEP is the United Nations' leading global environmental authority.

It supports international cooperation on climate change, biodiversity, pollution, resource management, and sustainable development.

Potential AI cooperation could include environmental data analysis, climate risk assessment, ecosystem monitoring, and air and water quality analysis.

Organization 04

UNHCR

Office of the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees

Refugee Protection and Forced Displacement

UNHCR works to protect and assist refugees, forcibly displaced people, and stateless persons.

Its responsibilities include international protection, humanitarian assistance, and support for durable solutions to displacement.

Potential AI cooperation could include humanitarian needs assessment, improved access to protection services, and analysis of displacement-related risks.

Any application must prioritize human rights, data protection, non-discrimination, and the safety of vulnerable populations.

Organization 05

IOM

International Organization for Migration

Migration and Displacement

IOM promotes humane and orderly migration and supports international cooperation on migration-related issues.

Its activities include migrant assistance, migration policy support, humanitarian response, and assistance to displaced populations.

Potential AI applications could include migration policy analysis, improved access to migrant services, and information support for humanitarian responses to displacement.

Organization 06

ILO

International Labour Organization

Employment and the Future of Work

ILO is the United Nations specialized agency promoting social justice, labour rights, decent work, and international labour standards.

Its tripartite structure brings together governments, employers, and workers.

Potential AI cooperation could include labour market analysis, future employment forecasting, skills development, employment service improvement, and protection of workers during technological transitions.

Organization 07

ITU

International Telecommunication Union

Emergency Communications and Early Warning

ITU is the United Nations specialized agency for digital technologies and information and communication technologies.

Its work includes telecommunications standardization, connectivity, digital infrastructure, and emergency communications.

Potential AI cooperation could include disaster communications, risk information dissemination, early-warning systems, and improved digital accessibility.

Organization 08

UNICEF

United Nations Children's Fund

Child Protection and Education

UNICEF works to protect children's rights and improve their health, education, safety, and overall well-being.

Its activities include child protection, education access, support for vulnerable children, and emergency assistance.

Potential AI applications could include educational accessibility, learning support, child welfare information analysis, and reducing digital inequalities.

Children's rights, privacy, safety, educational equity, and responsible AI governance must remain central considerations.

Organization 09

UNDP

United Nations Development Programme

Crisis Response and Sustainable Development

UNDP supports countries in addressing poverty, inequality, governance challenges, and sustainable development.

Its activities include institutional capacity development, crisis response, recovery, and support for the Sustainable Development Goals.

Potential AI cooperation could include development policy analysis, complex crisis assessment, public service improvement, and data-informed planning for sustainable development.

3. A Framework for International AI Cooperation

International organizations bring specialized knowledge, global networks, and experience in addressing complex development and humanitarian challenges.

Universities, research institutions, and technology companies may contribute research capabilities, AI expertise, and technological innovation.

The proposed Global AI Hub seeks to explore opportunities to connect these complementary strengths through practical international cooperation.

Joint Research

Identifying research questions related to global challenges and exploring collaborative research projects.

Responsible AI Pilots

Exploring AI pilot projects that respond to public-interest needs while evaluating safety, effectiveness, and ethical implications.

Policy and Knowledge Exchange

Supporting opportunities for international meetings, expert dialogue, policy learning, and the exchange of research findings.

Capacity Development

Exploring education, training, professional exchange, and capacity-building initiatives in international cooperation and responsible AI.

International Expertise + Responsible AI Innovation + Institutional Cooperation

The proposed framework seeks to encourage practical solutions that are consistent with the mandates and priorities of participating institutions.

4. Why Goyang Special City?

Goyang Special City is located in Gyeonggi Province, within the Seoul metropolitan region of the Republic of Korea.

Its existing assets include international convention and exhibition infrastructure centered on KINTEX, healthcare institutions such as the National Cancer Center, urban services, cultural facilities, and ecological resources including the Janghang Wetland.

These assets provide a foundation for exploring international cooperation in public health, environmental sustainability, disaster resilience, and AI-enabled innovation.

The Global AI Hub initiative seeks to connect international cooperation with new opportunities for research, innovation, professional employment, and sustainable regional development.

Actual economic and employment outcomes would depend on the scale of future institutional participation, the nature of cooperative projects, and the operational arrangements eventually established.

5. Three-Stage Development Strategy

The proposed strategy recognizes that successful international cooperation requires attention to initial establishment, sustainable operations, and long-term shared development.

Stage 1 — Initial Establishment

Assessing the requirements for suitable office facilities, transportation, housing, healthcare, education, and other essential services for potential participating institutions.

Stage 2 — Operational Support

Exploring coordination among relevant public authorities, universities, research institutions, businesses, and civil society to support international cooperation, AI research, pilot projects, and professional exchanges.

Stage 3 — Shared Growth

Seeking opportunities to contribute to the missions of participating organizations while encouraging regional innovation, professional employment, business activity, and sustainable economic development.

Initial Establishment → Operational Support → Shared Growth

Specific projects, funding arrangements, facilities, and institutional support measures would require consultation and formal decisions by the relevant authorities.

6. Institutional Status and Next Steps

The Global AI Hub in Goyang is presented as a proposed initiative supported by the Citizens' Promotion Committee.

The identification of nine international organizations reflects potential thematic relevance to the initiative.

It does not establish any formal partnership, endorsement, relocation decision, or commitment by these organizations.

Future engagement would require appropriate consultation with the organizations concerned and decisions through their respective institutional procedures.

The initiative seeks constructive dialogue, institutional cooperation, and responsible planning based on mutual interests and internationally recognized principles.

7. Public Engagement and International Cooperation

The Citizens' Promotion Committee seeks to raise public awareness of the Global AI Hub initiative and encourage constructive participation in discussions about Goyang's future development.

The initiative aims to connect citizen engagement with opportunities for international cooperation, research, and responsible AI innovation.
