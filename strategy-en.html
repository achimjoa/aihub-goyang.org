I. Strategic Vision

Goyang's strategy for hosting the Global AI Hub goes beyond attracting international organizations. It seeks to establish an environment in which international organizations can settle successfully, carry out their missions effectively, and grow together with the host city.

The Global AI Hub should not be understood merely as a building or office complex. It is envisioned as an international platform for cooperation in public-interest artificial intelligence, bringing together international organizations, governments, universities, research institutions, businesses and civil society.

Practical and achievable plans matter most.

The strategy emphasizes existing assets, realistic institutional cooperation, clear implementation responsibilities, financial planning and verifiable evidence, rather than abstract promises.

II. Three-Stage Development Strategy

1. Initial Settlement

Support international organizations in establishing their operations quickly and securely in Goyang by providing suitable office facilities, housing, transportation, healthcare, education and other essential services.

Creating a safe, convenient and welcoming living environment for international staff and their families is a central priority.

2. Operational Support

Connect the capabilities of Goyang and Gyeonggi Province, including public institutions, universities, research organizations, businesses and civil society, to support the practical work of international organizations.

Develop potential public-interest AI pilot projects in healthcare, food security, climate, disaster management, employment, education and social welfare.

3. Shared Growth

Help international organizations advance their institutional missions and the Sustainable Development Goals while enabling Goyang to develop into a city specializing in international cooperation and public-interest AI.

Build a sustainable ecosystem in which international organizations and local communities benefit from long-term cooperation.

III. International Cooperation in Public AI

The proposed Global AI Hub would enable nine international organizations to maintain their respective missions while benefiting from a shared public-interest AI cooperation platform.

1. Governance, Standards, Evaluation and Procurement

Support the development of governance frameworks, international standards, evaluation methods, procurement principles and operational practices for safe and trustworthy public AI.

2. Joint Design Across Specialized Domains

Enable international organizations to jointly design AI applications in healthcare, food security, climate action, migration, employment, disaster preparedness, child protection and complex crisis response.

3. Co-Development, Localization, Validation and Scaling

Bring together international organizations, partner countries, universities, research institutions and businesses to jointly develop AI solutions, adapt them to local conditions, validate their performance and support international deployment.

IV. Proposed Flagship AI Initiatives

AI-Enabled Primary Healthcare and Public Health

In alignment with the mission of the World Health Organization, Goyang proposes exploring AI-enabled primary healthcare, improved public-health services, infectious disease early warning and better access to essential care.

Potential local partners include the National Cancer Center and other healthcare institutions located in Goyang.

AI-Supported Complex Crisis Response

With UNDP as a potential cooperation partner, Goyang proposes exploring integrated AI analysis of disasters, climate change, food insecurity, migration, employment disruptions and social vulnerability.

The initiative could draw on Goyang's disaster-management, smart-city, environmental and social welfare capabilities.

V. Implementation and Cooperation Framework

Success in the competition to host the Global AI Hub requires more than ambitious plans. It requires practical feasibility, institutional coordination and credible evidence of implementation capacity.

Responsible Implementation Structure

Establish a practical cooperation framework involving Goyang, Gyeonggi Province, public institutions and private-sector experts, with clearly defined roles and responsibilities.

Broader Institutional Participation

Identify and confirm potential cooperation from universities, healthcare institutions, research organizations, businesses and civic groups.

Evidence-Based Preparation

Systematically prepare letters of intent, facility documentation, project plans, financial arrangements, operational frameworks and evidence of pilot-project feasibility.

From Planning to Implementation.
From Implementation to Results and Evidence.

Goyang seeks to develop a credible hosting strategy based on existing capabilities, realistic cooperation and achievable commitments.

VI. Our Goal

If Goyang is selected to host the Global AI Hub, the city and its civic partners will seek to help international organizations settle successfully, carry out their missions effectively, and establish a foundation for long-term shared growth.
