Goyang City's Declaration Ceremony for Hosting the UN Global AI Hub

📍 National Assembly Members' Office Building, Conference Room No. 2

Hosted by: National Assembly Members Kim Sung-hoe, Han Jun-ho, Lee Ki-heon, and Kim Young-hwan

Organized by: Goyang Special City and Goyang Research Institute

On August 25, 2026, Goyang Special City held a declaration ceremony at the National Assembly to demonstrate its commitment to hosting the UN Global AI Hub.

Lawmakers, local government representatives, and community members expressed their collective support for Goyang's bid.

Citizens' Forum on Peaceful Coexistence on the Korean Peninsula

📍 Gawaji Grand Auditorium, Ilsanseo-gu Office, Goyang

This event is documented as part of Goyang's civic history of peace and coexistence.
