Welcome to the official website of the Citizens' Promotion Committee for Attracting the Global AI Hub to Goyang Special City.

This website provides information about the proposed Global AI Hub, the Committee's activities, Goyang's location advantages, and opportunities for public participation.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Do I need to register to use this website?

General information can be accessed without registration. Some interactive functions may require login or additional permissions.

Q. Is the online support campaign the same as Committee registration?

No. The online support campaign allows individuals to express support for the proposed Global AI Hub.

Committee registration is a separate process for those wishing to participate in Committee activities.

Q. Does joining the online support campaign require a membership payment?

No. The online support campaign and supporting membership are separate participation programs.

Signing the support form does not itself constitute a commitment to pay a membership contribution.

Q. Does submitting a supporting membership application complete the payment?

No. Submitting the application and transferring funds are separate procedures.

Please verify the official payment information before making a transfer.

Q. Have the nine international organizations confirmed their relocation to Goyang?

No. The international organizations introduced on this website are discussed in connection with potential cooperation under the Global AI Hub initiative.

Their inclusion does not mean that participation, office establishment, or relocation to Goyang has been officially confirmed.

Q. Can a promotional banner be installed immediately after submitting an application?

No. Submitting an application does not automatically authorize banner installation.

Relevant laws, local regulations, and location-specific requirements must be observed.

7. Website Help

If the Website Does Not Open

Check your internet connection and website address. Try accessing the page again after a short interval.

If the Page Does Not Display Correctly

Refresh the page or try another browser.

If an Application Form Does Not Open

Select the application button again or open the form using another browser.

If Payment Account Information Is Not Displayed

Refresh the Citizen Participation Center and check again.

If the problem continues, do not transfer funds until the account information has been confirmed through official Committee guidance.

Personal Data Protection

Review the relevant personal data collection and use notice before submitting an application.

Do not post another person's personal information on public discussion boards.
