Your Participation Can Help Shape the Future

Why Civic Participation Matters

The Global AI Hub initiative is not simply about attracting international organizations or constructing new facilities.

It represents an opportunity to build an international cooperation platform that uses artificial intelligence to address global challenges, including disease, hunger, climate change, displacement, unemployment and educational inequality.

Meaningful civic participation can help ensure that the initiative reflects public needs, community values, human rights and sustainable development goals.

The Global AI Hub should be built not only for international organizations, but also with the participation of the communities it aims to serve.

Ways to Participate

1. Signature Campaign

Support the civic campaign calling for the Global AI Hub to be established in Goyang.

Information on official signature collection opportunities will be provided when available.

2. Volunteer Activities

Help with public events, information campaigns, community outreach and civic activities.

Volunteer opportunities will be announced through the Committee's official news channels.

3. Policy Suggestions

Share ideas on how Goyang can support international organizations, strengthen public-interest AI and contribute to global development.

A formal online submission channel is being considered.

4. Civic Organization Partnerships

Civic organizations, professional associations and community groups can explore opportunities for cooperation and joint public engagement.

5. Academic and Research Cooperation

Universities, research institutions and experts can contribute to policy discussions, research and potential public-interest AI projects.

6. International Cooperation

Overseas organizations and international partners are welcome to learn about Goyang's initiative and explore opportunities for dialogue and cooperation.

International Solidarity

Goyang's civic initiative seeks to connect local aspirations with global responsibilities.

We believe that artificial intelligence should serve humanity, protect vulnerable communities and contribute to sustainable development.

We welcome opportunities for dialogue with international organizations, universities, research institutions, civil society networks and communities around the world.
