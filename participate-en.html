The proposed Global AI Hub represents an important opportunity for Goyang Special City to develop as a center for international cooperation and AI-related industries.

The Citizens' Promotion Committee seeks to expand public understanding of the initiative and encourage voluntary participation by citizens, professionals, and civic organizations.

Your participation can help strengthen public support for bringing the Global AI Hub to Goyang Special City.

Participation Information

The Citizens' Promotion Committee operates several participation programs, including the public signature campaign, Committee registration, supporting membership, promotional banner applications, and vehicle sticker applications.

Each application is submitted through the relevant online form. Participants should review the purpose of the application and the information concerning the collection and use of personal data before applying.

Supporting membership contributions and promotional banner costs should be paid only to the designated bank accounts officially provided by the Committee.

Please verify the latest account details and payment conditions before making a transfer.

For information about personal data protection, please refer to the Privacy Policy (Korean) .
